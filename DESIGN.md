# InstantBible — Design System

> Quiet faith for everyday life.

**Reference:** ElevenLabs visual language as documented by Refero Styles  
**Reference URL:** https://styles.refero.design/style/031056ff-7af1-46db-8daa-115f731c5d26

## Thesis
InstantBible should not look like a generic Bible reader, church app, meditation clone, or gamified streak product. It should feel like a quiet editorial space where Scripture becomes practical.

Core experience:

**Scripture → Understand → Act → Reflect → Habit**

## Principles
1. Calm over stimulation.
2. Editorial over app chrome.
3. The Word is the interface.
4. One obvious action per screen.
5. Practice over consumption.
6. Personalization should feel pastoral, not algorithmic.

## Components
- light editorial headings
- full-pill primary actions
- 20–24px cards
- hairline borders
- almost no shadows
- narrow reading width
- saturated color only as a meaningful signal

## Daily ritual
1. Arrive
2. Scripture
3. Understand
4. Act
5. Reflect

No confetti. No streak anxiety. No generic purple gradients. No literal copying of ElevenLabs assets or layouts.

## AI guardrail
AI may help explain context, suggest reflection questions, summarize journal themes, or personalize sequencing. It must not present generated text as divine revelation, prophecy, pastoral authority, or a substitute for qualified clergy or professional care.

Bible text used in production must follow the licensing terms of the selected translation.

## Product test
> Does this help the user move from reading to living one useful biblical idea today?
